The maths behind each calculator

EMI and amortization

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ [(1 + r)ⁿ − 1], with P principal, r monthly rate, n months.

SIP future value

FV = P × [((1 + i)ⁿ − 1) ÷ i] × (1 + i).

GST

Add GST: total = amount × (1 + rate ÷ 100). Remove: pre-tax = total ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100).
