Mathematical Reference & Methodology

How Our Indian Financial, Tax & Health Formulas Work

Every utility on NeiharDario uses transparent, standard mathematical equations audited against Reserve Bank of India (RBI) reducing-balance conventions, Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) GST rules, and Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) health guidelines:

  • Reducing-Balance Loan EMI Formula: EMI = [P × r × (1 + r)^n] / [(1 + r)^n − 1], where P is loan principal, r is monthly interest rate (Annual Rate / 12 / 100), and n is total monthly installments.
  • Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) Annuity-Due Formula: FV = P × [((1 + i)^n − 1) / i] × (1 + i), accounting for monthly compounding from day one of each installment.
  • Forward & Reverse GST Extraction: Adding GST multiplies base price by (1 + Rate/100). Removing GST from an inclusive MRP divides the gross invoice by (1 + Rate/100) so you never over-subtract tax.
  • Indian Asian-Indian BMI Consensus: Computes Weight (kg) / [Height (m)]² and evaluates risk at 18.0–22.9 (Normal), 23.0–24.9 (Overweight), and ≥25.0 (Obese).

Frequently Asked Questions (7 Essential Guides)

1. How is my monthly loan EMI and amortization schedule calculated? ▼
Your Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is calculated using the standard reducing-balance compound interest formula: EMI = [P × r × (1 + r)^n] / [(1 + r)^n − 1], where P is the principal loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (Annual Rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), and n is the total number of monthly installments. In the amortization schedule, each month's interest is calculated on the remaining opening principal balance (Opening Balance × r), and the remaining portion of the EMI (EMI − Monthly Interest) goes toward reducing your principal balance.
2. How does compounding work in a Monthly SIP Calculator? ▼
A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) uses the annuity-due compound interest formula: FV = P × [((1 + i)^n − 1) / i] × (1 + i), where P is your monthly investment, i is the periodic monthly return rate (Annual Return Rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), and n is the total number of months (Years × 12). Each monthly contribution compounds independently for the remaining months of your tenure, allowing returns to generate further returns.
3. How do I add or remove GST from an invoice, and where can I verify official GST rates? ▼
To add GST to a pre-tax base amount (GST Exclusive), multiply the base price by (GST Rate ÷ 100) and add it to the base price. To remove GST from a tax-inclusive MRP or invoice (Reverse GST), divide the gross amount by [1 + (GST Rate ÷ 100)] to find the exact pre-tax base price. For Intra-State transactions within the same state, total GST is split 50:50 into CGST and SGST; for Inter-State transactions across state borders, 100% is charged as IGST. You can verify current HSN and SAC tax rates directly on the official Indian GST Portal at https://www.gst.gov.in.
4. Why does the BMI Calculator show different overweight and obesity cut-offs for Indians? ▼
Clinical guidelines issued by the Indian Ministry of Health, the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR), and the Consensus Statement for Asian Indians establish stricter, lower BMI thresholds than the standard WHO table because South Asians exhibit higher visceral abdominal fat and higher insulin resistance at lower body weights. Specifically, Indian guidelines define Normal BMI as 18.0 to 22.9 kg/m² (vs. 18.5 to 24.9 in WHO), Overweight as 23.0 to 24.9 kg/m² (vs. 25.0 to 29.9 in WHO), and Obesity as ≥ 25.0 kg/m² (vs. ≥ 30.0 in WHO).
